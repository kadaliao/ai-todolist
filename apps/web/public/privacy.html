Docket 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 13 日

Docket 不收集你的任何数据。没有账号系统，没有分析、统计或广告 SDK，开发者也没有接收数据的服务器。

你的任务存在哪里

所有任务、项目和设置都保存在你的设备上，离线也能完整使用。iPhone 的小组件和分享扩展通过系统的 App Group 在本机读写，数据不会离开设备。

同步

只有你在“设置 → 同步”里填写了服务器地址和令牌，数据才会同步到那台服务器。服务器是你自己部署和管理的 Docket 服务器（开源，可自托管），开发者无法访问。

AI 功能

AI 功能是可选的。使用时，你提交的文字会经你自己的 Docket 服务器转发给你在服务器上配置的模型提供方，处理方式受该提供方的隐私政策约束。不使用 AI 就不会发送任何内容。

权限

  • 通知：用于按时发送你设置的提醒，由系统在本机调度。
  • 麦克风与语音识别：只在你主动口述任务时使用。

儿童

Docket 不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

变更与联系

本政策如有变更，会更新在本页并修改生效日期。有问题请在 GitHub 提交 issue。

Docket Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Docket does not collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics or advertising SDKs, and no developer server that receives your data.

Where your tasks live

Your tasks, projects and settings are stored on your device and work fully offline. On iPhone, the widgets and the share extension read and write through the system App Group on the same device; nothing leaves it.

Sync

Your data is synced only if you enter a server address and token in Settings → Sync. That server is a Docket server you run yourself (open source, self-hosted); the developer has no access to it.

AI features

AI features are optional. When you use them, the text you submit goes through your own Docket server to the model provider configured on that server, and is handled under that provider's privacy policy. Nothing is sent if you don't use AI.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to deliver the reminders you set, scheduled by the system on your device.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: only while you dictate a task.

Children

Docket collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
